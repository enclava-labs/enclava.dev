How it works

From docker push
to confidential, in four steps.

You give us a container. We give you a confidential application — reachable on a TLS endpoint, with cryptographic proof of what's inside. Scroll through a deploy.

STEP 01

Describe your app

One declarative file: image, resources, domains, persistent volumes. Same mental model you already have for Docker Compose or any modern PaaS.

STEP 02

We verify & provision

The platform pulls your image, verifies its signature, generates the orchestration to run it inside a TEE on AMD SEV-SNP, and provisions encrypted storage with keys only the TEE holds.

signed imageencrypted volumeisolated network
STEP 03

Your app boots inside a TEE

The container runs in a hardware-isolated trusted execution environment where memory is encrypted at runtime. Even with full root on the host, the cloud operator cannot read your process memory, your filesystem, or your traffic.

STEP 04

Secrets flow directly into the TEE

API keys, DB credentials, signing keys — encrypted on your laptop, opened only inside the running TEE. They never touch our control plane in plaintext.

sealed on your machineopened in-enclave only

The platform

Everything a cloud gives you.
None of the trust.

Deploy with the ergonomics of a modern PaaS. Get hardware-grade confidentiality you can prove to your customers, your auditors, and yourself.

01 · Runtime

Encrypted in use

Your container runs inside a TEE where memory is encrypted at runtime. Even with full root on the host, the cloud operator cannot read your process memory, filesystem, or traffic.

02 · Proof

Remote attestation

Every app emits a signed statement from the hardware describing exactly which code is running. Anyone can verify it before sending data.

measurement sha256:9f2a…c41d
policy SEV-SNP · v1.55
launch amd-signed
03 · Secrets

Direct-to-enclave

API keys, DB URLs, signing keys — encrypted on your laptop, opened only inside the running TEE. Never plaintext in our control plane.

04 · Storage

Encrypted volumes

Persistent storage with keys held only by the TEE. Ops never needs root in your data.

05 · Network

TLS inside the enclave

Traffic terminates inside the TEE. The network path is untrusted — and that's fine.
